# AraGrow – RSTC Client Partnership Methodology

Source: https://aragrow.me/r-s-t-c/

The RSTC Method is David Arago's proprietary framework for building and maintaining strong client relationships. It is a structured, no-pressure consultative approach centered on four pillars.

---

## The Four Pillars: R-S-T-C

| Letter | Pillar | Meaning |
|--------|--------|---------|
| R | **Respect** | The foundation – treating clients with dignity and valuing their perspective |
| S | **Safety** | Empowering clients to make decisions without pressure |
| T | **Trust** | The bridge that enables deep partnership |
| C | **Client** | Keeping the client's interests and readiness at the center |

## RSTC Philosophy Summary

The RSTC method is rooted in a belief that **long-term client relationships** are more valuable than quick sales. Key principles:

1. **No pressure** – Clients make decisions on their own timeline
2. **Value first** – Lead every interaction with genuine insight or help
3. **Respect autonomy** – The client knows their business; you know technology
4. **Stay the course** – Consistent, patient relationship-building wins
5. **Read the room** – Adapt to client signals, both verbal and non-verbal

This approach differentiates AraGrow from transactional IT vendors by positioning David Arago as a trusted advisor rather than a salesperson.
