## David Arago's Professional Philosophy (Cross-Post Themes)

Across multiple blog posts, David Arago's professional philosophy emerges consistently:

1. **Continuous learning** – Always exploring new tools, languages, and technologies (AI, LLMs, local models)
2. **Human-first approach** – Technology serves people, not the other way around
3. **Authentic consulting** – No-pressure, value-first client relationships (RSTC)
4. **Personal growth** – Age is not a barrier to learning new skills (building AI tools at 56)
5. **Work-life integration** – Designing a career that supports life, not just productivity
6. **Social responsibility** – Using tech skills for community good (AfricAI)
7. **Transparency** – Sharing lessons learned openly via blog and community channels
